# Roadmap

## v0.1

Rules, weighted scoring, capability filtering, cost and latency constraints, fallbacks, retries, circuit breaker, adapters, explain API, CLI, proxy, playground, evals, replay and shadow decisions.

## v0.2

Python SDK if demand supports it, Redis health and budget stores, stronger catalog provenance, policy visualization and framework integrations.

## v0.3

Experimental contextual bandits, calibrated confidence, opt-in online learning, A/B testing and drift detection.

## Future

Kubernetes or Envoy integration, self-hosted inference adapters, regional routing, enterprise policy bundles, MCP-aware routing and a quality-observation marketplace without misleading rankings.

Learning-based features remain experimental until workload evidence and opt-in safety controls exist.
