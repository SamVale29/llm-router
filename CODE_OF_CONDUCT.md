# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful and technically rigorous community.

Expected behavior includes empathy, constructive disagreement, privacy awareness and good-faith collaboration. Harassment, discrimination, doxxing, threats, spam and deliberate exposure of secrets or user data are not acceptable.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Report a concern privately to the security contact in SECURITY.md.
